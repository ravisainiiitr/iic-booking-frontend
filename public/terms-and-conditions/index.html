1. Scope

These terms apply to bookings of instruments and facilities of the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee made through the IIT Roorkee Equipment Booking Portal. Each facility may have its own rules, shown on its page in the portal, which also apply.

2. Eligibility and accounts

  • You need an approved account. IIT Roorkee users sign in with Channel i; other users register and are approved before booking.
  • The information you give must be correct and kept up to date. Your account is for you alone: do not share your sign-in.
  • Bookings by students may need their supervisor's or wallet owner's approval, as shown in the portal.

3. Bookings and confirmation

  • A booking is confirmed when the portal shows it as Booked. A booking awaiting payment is not confirmed.
  • An online payment counts only when the payment gateway confirms it to the portal. A message in your browser alone is not confirmation.
  • A facility may decline or reschedule a booking that it cannot carry out, for example when an instrument is down, and will tell you through the portal.

4. Payment

  • Charges are those shown in the portal before you pay; see Pricing.
  • You may pay from your portal wallet, online through the Razorpay payment gateway, or by another method offered for your category, such as a bank transfer verified by the institute.
  • Online payments are collected for the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee in the account held by DEAN SRIC IIT ROORKEE. Card, UPI and bank details are entered only in the payment gateway's checkout.
  • A receipt is available for each confirmed online payment. Where a GST tax invoice applies, it is issued by SRIC, IIT Roorkee.

5. Using the facility

  • Follow the facility's rules, safety instructions and the directions of its staff.
  • Arrive, or submit samples, by the time the portal shows. A slot that is not used may be marked as not utilised.
  • Submit only samples that are correctly described and safe to handle, with any hazard declared in advance. The facility may refuse a sample it cannot handle safely.

6. Cancellation and rescheduling

Cancellation and rescheduling follow the Cancellation and Refund Policy.

7. Prohibited use

  • Giving false information, using another person's account, or booking on behalf of someone not entitled to the rate charged
  • Misusing or damaging equipment, or ignoring safety rules
  • Attempting to interfere with the portal or its payments

The institute may suspend an account or refuse bookings for such use.

8. Service limitations

Instruments can break down or need maintenance. When this affects your booking, the portal offers rescheduling or a refund as set out in the Cancellation and Refund Policy.

9. Questions and grievances

Contact us as set out on Contact Us. Matters are handled under the rules of the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee.
