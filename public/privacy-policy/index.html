1. Introduction and scope

The IIT Roorkee Equipment Booking Portal at https://equip.iitr.ac.in/ is the institute-wide online platform for booking laboratory equipment of the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee (IIT Roorkee). It is used by students, staff and faculty of the institute, and by users from other academic, government and industry organisations.

This policy explains what personal information the portal and its mobile app handle, why, who else processes it, how long it is kept and what you can ask us to do. Instructions for asking us to delete information are on the Data Deletion Instructions page.

3. Information we collect

What we hold about you depends on who you are and which parts of the portal you use.

Account and profile information

  • Name, email address, password (stored only in hashed form), gender, user category, and department or organisation.
  • Where relevant: employee or enrolment number, date of birth, programme or degree, branch, designation, joining and graduation dates, supervisor, and a profile picture.
  • If you sign in through the institute's Channel i service, identity details it provides, such as your user ID, enrolment number, degree, department, designation and programme dates.
  • Account status, such as email verification, approvals, suspension and account end dates.
  • Your ORCID iD and publication details, if you add or confirm them.

Contact details

  • Mobile numbers and email address.
  • Postal, billing and shipping addresses, and a GSTIN, where you give them for invoices or dispatch.

Equipment booking information

  • The equipment, slots, charges, status and history of your bookings, and who made them.
  • Sample and analysis details you enter, notes, and sample tracking and return details.
  • Results and data released to you, your ratings and feedback, and waitlist and cancellation records.

Payment and transaction references

  • Wallet balances and transactions, recharge requests, invoices and refunds.
  • Payment gateway order, payment, refund and settlement references, and payment status, method and amount (see section 8).
  • Payment receipts you upload, and bank account details or a UPI ID you give for refunds.

Uploaded samples, documents and research files

  • Documents you upload, which can include identity, address or institution proofs, approval letters and support ticket attachments.
  • Design files for fabrication bookings, such as STL and DXF files.
  • Files, folders, project details, research group updates and activity records in My Research, and files in remote analysis workspaces.

The portal cannot tell what personal information you place inside a file. Please upload only what the work requires.

WhatsApp contact details and notification preferences

  • The WhatsApp number you give when you opt in, and whether you are opted in or out.
  • A history of each opt-in and opt-out, with its time and source.
  • A record of each message sent to you and its delivery status, and messages you send to the portal's WhatsApp number (see section 7).

Communications and calls

  • Records of emails and notifications sent to you, and your support tickets.
  • For in-portal voice calls: who called whom, the related booking or equipment, the time, duration and outcome. Call audio is not recorded.
  • Questions you type into the portal's assistant, where it is available to you.

Technical information and usage logs

  • Server logs and security records, which can include IP address, browser details and the time of a request.
  • For the mobile app: device name, platform, app version, last-used time and address, and a device token for push notifications.
  • Activity and audit records of important actions, such as changes to bookings, wallets and settings.

4. Why we collect it

  • To create and verify accounts, and to apply the rules and charges for each user category.
  • To take and manage bookings, samples, results and refunds.
  • To collect payments, issue invoices and keep financial records.
  • To send booking, account and service notifications.
  • To let you store and share research files, and to answer support requests.
  • To report on and plan the use of the institute's equipment.
  • To secure the portal, investigate problems, keep audit records and meet institutional and legal obligations.

5. How it is processed and used

Your information is stored in the portal's database and file storage, and is used by the portal to carry out the actions you request and the automatic steps they involve, such as checking eligibility, calculating charges, sending reminders and updating statuses. Access inside the portal depends on the role of the person signed in.

6. Booking and administrative workflows

People at IIT Roorkee see the information they need for their role. Depending on the booking, this includes:

  • lab operators and officers in charge of the equipment you book;
  • department and portal administrators;
  • the institute's accounts and finance staff, for payments, invoices and refunds;
  • your supervisor or wallet owner, where a booking, approval or wallet is linked to them;
  • people you choose to share a research workspace or group with.

7. WhatsApp notifications

The portal can send booking notifications through the WhatsApp Business Cloud API provided by Meta. They are optional, and email and in-portal notifications continue regardless. WhatsApp notifications are being introduced gradually and may not yet be available for your account.

What may be sent

  • Booking confirmations: the booking ID, equipment and first slot.
  • Booking cancellations, including refunds: the booking ID, equipment and first slot.
  • Booking completion: the booking ID, equipment and a link to My Bookings.

The portal does not currently send reminders or other status updates on WhatsApp. If these are added, this policy will be updated.

Consent and opting out

  • You opt in yourself under Profile → WhatsApp notifications, and confirm the number to use. Nothing is sent to you on WhatsApp unless you have opted in.
  • You can opt out at any time in the same place, or by replying STOP to our WhatsApp number. Messages still waiting to be sent are then cancelled.
  • If you opted in earlier and then replied STOP, you can opt back in by replying START.

What we store

  • Your opt-in status and number, and a history of consent changes, with time and source.
  • For each message sent: the number, template, details included in the message and delivery status (such as sent, delivered, read or failed).
  • Messages you send to our WhatsApp number, if the number has an opt-in record. These can be read only by the portal's Main Administrator. Messages from numbers with no opt-in record are not stored.
  • A technical record that each event from Meta has been handled, which holds no message content or phone number.

Retention of these records is described in section 11. Meta and WhatsApp also process messages under their own terms and privacy policies.

8. Payment processing and transaction information

Online payments are processed by Razorpay. Card, UPI and net banking details are entered in Razorpay's checkout, not in a portal form, and the portal has no field for storing a full card number or CVV. Older online transactions may have been processed through SBIePay.

The portal keeps payment order, payment, refund and settlement references, along with status, method, amounts and bank references. It also keeps the responses the payment gateway returns, which may contain details such as the payer's contact information or limited information about the payment method.

The portal also holds wallet records, recharge requests and receipts, invoices, and any bank account details or UPI ID you give for refunds. Bank account numbers are shown masked, except to the staff who process refunds and in the institute's refund requests.

Payment gateways process your information under their own terms and privacy policies.

9. File storage and document handling

Uploaded files are stored in Amazon Web Services (AWS) S3 cloud storage. They are made available through the portal, or through time-limited links, to users whose role or sharing permits it.

Deleting a file in My Research removes it from the portal for users, but does not by itself erase the stored copy. See section 11 and the Data Deletion Instructions.

11. Data retention and deletion

The portal does not automatically delete an account or its history when a person leaves the institute or stops using the portal. An account may be suspended at the end of a programme or employment, and its records remain. Unless a shorter period is listed below, information is generally kept while the account exists and for as long as institutional, financial, legal or audit needs require.

Registrations not verified by emailRemoved automatically after the verification time limit.

Physical samples are handled under each equipment's sample rules, and the portal records when a sample is disposed of. To ask for your information to be deleted, follow the Data Deletion Instructions. The portal has no self-service option for deleting an account.

12. Data security measures

The measures in place include:

  • encrypted HTTPS connections to the portal;
  • passwords stored only as hashes, and limits on repeated failed sign-ins;
  • sign-in required, with role-based permission checks on the server;
  • uploaded files kept private, and opened through permission checks or time-limited links;
  • signature checks on payment gateway and WhatsApp webhooks;
  • mobile app sign-in tokens stored only in hashed form, and the ability to revoke them;
  • audit records of important actions, and regular database backups.

No online system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please keep your password private and tell us at once if you suspect misuse of your account.

13. Your choices and how to raise a privacy request

  • You can view and correct much of your profile in the portal. Some details come from the institute's records, and may need to be corrected there.
  • You can turn WhatsApp notifications on or off at any time.
  • You can ask for a copy of your information, for its correction or deletion, or raise a concern, by emailing equip@iitr.ac.in from your registered email address.

We may need to verify your identity before acting, and some information may have to be kept (see section 11). This policy does not limit any right you have under applicable law.

14. Cookies and technical information

The portal's web application does not include advertising or analytics tracking code. It uses the following for its own operation:

  • Cookies: session and security (CSRF) cookies set by the server, and a cookie that remembers whether a side menu is open.
  • Browser storage: your sign-in token, basic account details, cached wallet and notification data, and preferences, kept on your own device. Signing out removes your sign-in token.

The payment checkout, fonts and embedded content such as videos are served by third parties, who may set their own cookies or receive your IP address when those features are used. These two policy pages set no cookies, and load no scripts or third-party content.
