1. In short

  • The earlier you cancel, the more you get back: everything more than 48 hours before your first slot, half between 48 and 24 hours, nothing in the last 24 hours.
  • When you cancel, the return shipping charge is not refunded, nor is the GST on it.
  • If the facility cannot carry out your booking, you get everything back, return shipping included.
  • Refunds go to your portal wallet straight away. They are not sent back to the card, UPI account or bank account you paid from.

2. If you cancel

You can cancel your booking yourself from My bookings until your first slot starts. The portal shows how much you will get back before you confirm. What you get back depends on how long before the first slot you cancel:

When you cancelAnalysis charges refunded
More than 48 hours before the first slotAll of them (100%)
Between 48 and 24 hours beforeHalf (50%)
Less than 24 hours beforeNone
  • Weekly booking window: for a booking made in the weekly booking window, cancelling before the Wednesday 20:30 cut-off gives a full refund.
  • Part of a booking: you can cancel some of the slots or samples. The refund is then worked out for the part you cancel.
  • After the deadline: once you can no longer cancel yourself, message the lab from your booking's details page.

3. Return shipping and GST

Return shipping charge

If you asked for your samples to be sent back, your booking includes a return shipping charge.

  • When you cancel (at any time): the return shipping charge and the GST on it are not refunded.
  • When the facility cannot carry out your booking (section 5): the return shipping charge is refunded, with its GST.

GST

  • Whatever part of a charge is refunded, the GST paid on that part is refunded with it. GST on the parts that are not refunded is not refunded either.
  • Exception: if you have already asked for a GST invoice for the booking, the portal refunds only the amount before GST. The GST is then settled by SRIC against your invoice (a credit note).

4. A worked example

Say your booking was: analysis charges Rs. 800 + return shipping Rs. 200 + GST at 18% Rs. 180 = you paid Rs. 1,180.

What happensRefundedNot refunded

You cancel between 48 and 24 hours before Rs. 472 (half the analysis, Rs. 400 + its GST Rs. 72) Rs. 708
You cancel less than 24 hours before Rs. 0 Rs. 1,180
The facility cannot carry out the booking Rs. 1,180 (everything, shipping included) Nothing

If you had already asked for a GST invoice, the first line would refund Rs. 800, and SRIC would settle the Rs. 144 GST against your invoice.

5. If the facility cannot do your booking

Sometimes the facility cannot carry out a booking: the instrument is down or under maintenance, the operator is not available, the analysis is not possible, or your files or samples cannot be processed. Then:

  • There is no penalty. You can reschedule, or cancel for a full refund, return shipping included.
  • If your booking was moved because of maintenance, you can still cancel for a full refund until the deadline shown for the new slot.

6. Where your refund goes, and when

  • Portal wallet: refunds are credited to your wallet at once, and you can use the balance for later bookings.
  • Your bank account: if the instrument itself is down or under maintenance, you can choose to have the refund paid to your bank account instead. Bank refunds are sent with the institute's weekly refund batch on the first working day of the week. How long the money then takes to arrive depends on the banks.
  • Wallet balance to bank: where the Wallet page offers it, you can ask for your wallet balance to be paid to your bank account. The request is checked and then paid by the institute.
  • Refunds are never sent back automatically to the card, UPI account or bank account used for an online payment.

7. Problems with an online payment

  • Failed payment: you are not charged.
  • Money taken but the payment is not confirmed: if the payment was not completed, the payment gateway returns the money to you automatically. If it was completed, the institute either matches it to your booking or refunds it.
  • Charged twice for one booking: the second payment is refunded by the institute once it has been checked.
  • Convenience fee: if a payment gateway convenience fee was charged, that fee and its GST are not refunded.

8. IIT Roorkee users

IIT Roorkee users can cancel until a set number of hours before the first slot: 48 hours, unless the instrument's page says otherwise. The deadline is shown in the portal. When you cancel before it, the charge for the cancelled part goes back to the wallet that paid it.

9. Questions

For a question about a payment or a refund, contact the Accounts In Charge of the department that runs the instrument. Your booking's details page shows the department. Please give your booking reference and your payment reference (receipt number or Razorpay payment ID).

For a question about the booking itself, message the lab from your booking's details page, or see Contact Us on the portal.
