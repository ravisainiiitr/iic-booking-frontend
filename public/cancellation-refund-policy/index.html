1. In short

  • Refunds for cancelled bookings are credited to your portal wallet.
  • Where the policy below allows it, a refund can be paid to your bank account through the institute's offline process.
  • Refunds are not sent back automatically to the card, UPI account or bank account used for an online payment.

2. Who can cancel

  • You can cancel your own booking from My bookings until the cancellation deadline shown for it.
  • After the deadline, contact the facility; facility staff can cancel a booking at any time.
  • You can cancel some of the slots or samples of a booking; the refund is then worked out for the cancelled part.

3. IIT Roorkee users

  • You can cancel until a set number of hours before the first slot. The number is set for each instrument (48 hours unless the instrument says otherwise) and the deadline is shown in the portal.
  • When you cancel before the deadline, the charge for the cancelled part is credited back to the wallet that paid it.

4. External users

For bookings by external users, the refund depends on how long before the first slot you cancel:

When you cancelRefund of service charges
More than 48 hours before the first slotFull refund
Between 48 and 24 hours before50% refund
Less than 24 hours beforeNo refund

For bookings in the weekly booking window, cancelling before the Wednesday 20:30 cut-off gives a full refund.

The return shipping charge and its GST are not refunded. The GST on the refunded part is refunded, unless a GST invoice has already been requested for the booking, in which case the tax is settled with that invoice.

The refund is credited to your portal wallet at once.

5. When the facility cannot carry out the booking

  • If the instrument is down, under maintenance or the analysis is not possible, there is no penalty. You can choose to reschedule, or to cancel for a full refund.
  • If the instrument itself is not working, you can choose a refund to your wallet at once, or to your bank account. Bank refunds are sent with the institute's weekly refund batch on the first working day of the week and are marked complete when the money reaches your account.
  • If a facility rejects your files or samples as not feasible, you can cancel with a full refund.
  • If a booking is moved because of maintenance, you can cancel with a full refund until the deadline shown for the new slot.

6. Online payments

  • The payment gateway convenience fee and the GST on it are not part of the booking charge and are not included in wallet refunds.
  • A failed payment is not charged. If money leaves your account but the payment is not confirmed to the portal, the payment gateway reverses an uncaptured payment automatically; a captured payment is matched to your booking or refunded by the institute.
  • If you are charged twice for the same booking, the second payment is recorded and refunded by the institute after it is verified.

7. Wallet balance

A wallet balance can be used for later bookings. Where the Wallet page offers it, you can ask for a wallet balance to be paid to your bank account; the request is checked and paid through the institute's offline process.

8. How long it takes

  • Wallet refunds: at the time of cancellation.
  • Bank refunds: sent with the next weekly refund batch; the time for the money to reach your account depends on the banks.

9. Questions and disputes

For a payment or refund question, write to arsric@iitr.ac.in with your booking reference and payment reference. For a booking question, see Contact Us on the portal.
