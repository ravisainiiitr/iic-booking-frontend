1. Introduction

This page explains how to ask for personal information associated with the IIT Roorkee Equipment Booking Portal (https://equip.iitr.ac.in/) to be deleted. The portal is the institute-wide equipment booking platform of the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee.

The portal does not have a button that deletes your account. Requests are made by email and reviewed by the portal's administrators. Deletion is not immediate, and some information may have to be kept. We will tell you what was deleted and what was kept.

To stop WhatsApp messages you do not need to make a request: reply STOP on WhatsApp, or turn them off under Profile → WhatsApp notifications.

2. What you can ask us to delete

  • Your account and profile details, including contact details and your profile picture.
  • Saved addresses, and bank or UPI details you gave for refunds.
  • Documents you uploaded.
  • My Research files, folders and workspaces that belong to you.
  • Your WhatsApp number, and WhatsApp notification and message records.
  • Support tickets, messages you sent, and your feedback.
  • Mobile app device records and notification tokens.

Each request is reviewed individually. Asking for deletion does not guarantee that every item can be deleted (see section 8).

3. How to make a request

  1. Send an email to equip@iitr.ac.in with the subject Data deletion request. Please send it from the email address registered on the portal, if you can.
  2. Say who you are and what you want deleted, using the list in section 5.
  3. Wait for our acknowledgement, and reply if we ask you to confirm your identity.
  4. We review your request, and tell you about anything that must be kept, or any open booking, balance or refund to settle first.
  5. We carry out the deletion and email you the outcome.

4. Where to send it

The only official address for deletion requests is equip@iitr.ac.in.

5. What to include

  • Your full name, as on your portal account.
  • The email address registered on the portal.
  • Your user category (for example student, faculty or external user), and your department or organisation.
  • What you want deleted: your whole account, or particular items such as named files or documents.
  • For WhatsApp records: the number you used, with its country code.
  • Any booking references that relate to your request.
Do not put passwords, one-time passwords, card numbers or bank details in your request. Do not send identity documents unless we specifically ask.

6. Verifying your identity

We must confirm that a request comes from the account holder, so that nobody can delete someone else's information. We will normally check that it came from the registered email address, and may ask you to confirm by replying from that address.

If you no longer have access to that address, tell us in your request. We may ask for further details that match the account. We may decline a request if we cannot verify it.

7. How requests are reviewed and processed

Administrators of the portal check the account for:

  • bookings in progress, samples held and results not yet released;
  • wallet balances, pending payments, refunds and invoices;
  • files or workspaces shared with other people;
  • records that must be kept (see section 8).

Open items may need to be completed first. Where an account cannot be fully removed, we can disable it so that it can no longer be used, and delete or remove the items that do not have to be kept. We will explain what was done and why.

8. Information that may be retained

Some information may be retained where required by institutional policy, or financial, audit or applicable legal obligations, or to resolve disputes. This may include:

  • booking, payment, wallet, refund, invoice and settlement records, with the name and references needed to make them meaningful;
  • results and records that relate to use of institute equipment, or that other people or projects rely on;
  • consent, security and audit records.

Information held by payment gateways, Meta, and other independent providers is handled under their own policies, and you may need to contact them directly. See the Privacy Policy for the list of providers.

9. Uploaded files and AWS S3 objects

Uploaded files are stored in Amazon Web Services (AWS) S3 storage.

  • Deleting a file in My Research yourself removes it from the portal for users, but the stored copy stays in S3. To have the stored copy erased, please make a request and name the files or workspace.
  • Removing a record from the portal does not always remove its stored file, so approved file deletions are carried out as a separate step in S3.
  • Fabrication design files (STL, DXF) are removed from storage automatically after the booking is completed.
  • Links to a file that were issued earlier can keep working until they expire, for a period of up to 7 days.
  • Files in a workspace you share with others, and results released for a booking, are reviewed before any deletion.

10. WhatsApp notification records

  • Opting out takes effect as soon as it is processed, and cancels messages still waiting to be sent. Your opt-out is recorded, so that we do not message you again.
  • Incoming messages and Meta event records are deleted automatically after 90 days. You can ask for earlier deletion of your incoming messages.
  • Consent history and sent-message records hold your number, the time and, for messages, the content and delivery status. They are not deleted automatically, and may remain after an account is removed, as a record of consent and delivery. You can ask us to review them.
  • Your own WhatsApp chat, and information held by Meta, are outside the portal. You can delete the chat in WhatsApp. Meta's handling is covered by its own terms and policies.

11. Backups and audit logs

  • Backups: the portal's database is backed up regularly. Individual records are not removed from existing backups. Deleted information can remain in a backup until it expires on the rolling backup schedule, and backups are used only for recovery and integrity checks.
  • Audit and security logs: records of important actions and technical logs are kept to protect the portal and meet audit needs, and may continue to refer to your account or actions after a deletion.

12. Expected acknowledgement and processing timelines

We aim to:

  • acknowledge your request within 7 working days; and
  • complete the review and tell you the outcome within 30 days of verifying your identity.

These are targets, not guarantees. Requests may take longer when open bookings, payments, refunds or institutional checks are involved, and we will tell you if so.
